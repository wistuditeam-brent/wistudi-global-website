What Wistudi is

An authoring and publishing layer around specialist learning technology.

The connection itself is only one part of the value. Wistudi gives people the environment to compose complete learning experiences, publish them as reusable learning products, and distribute them across individual, team and organisation contexts.

01

Compose

Put content, video, activities, assessment, reflection, resources and partner technology into one designed learning journey.

Your technology becomes part of the experience.
02

Publish

Turn authored work into reusable lessons, courses, curricula, interactive stories, worksheets, handouts and supporting learning resources.

Your technology can become part of what people publish.
03

Scale

Move from one educator or publisher to teams, schools, training organisations and wider institutional deployment without changing the underlying learning model.

Bottom-up adoption and top-down deployment can coexist.
A different kind of integration

The difference is what happens around your technology.

A conventional integration can solve the launch. Wistudi is designed to solve the experience around the launch — before, during and after the specialist capability is used.

Publishing changes the proposition

Your technology can become part of what people publish.

Wistudi is not only a place to create a course shell. Educators, trainers, publishers and organisations can build reusable learning products around specialist technology, then publish and distribute those products through the Wistudi ecosystem.

Why companies integrate with Wistudi

More than another place to launch your application.

Wistudi gives specialist technology a larger authored, published and distributed context while allowing the partner to remain recognisable and focused on what it does best.

01

Part of the lesson, not the destination

Place partner capability between context, content, activities, assessment, reflection and resources instead of isolating it as the end point.

02

More published contexts

The same capability can support different lessons, courses, programmes, curricula and authored learning products.

03

Your identity stays visible

The specialist product can remain branded and recognisable while Wistudi supplies the surrounding authoring, publishing and learning layer.

04

Creators can build around it

Once connected, educators and publishers can create new learning experiences around the capability without the partner rebuilding every lesson.

Different partners. One ecosystem.

There are different ways to build with Wistudi.

LTI is a major technical pathway, but a partnership does not have to begin with engineering. The relationship can start where the commercial and learning value makes sense.

Technology partners

Bring specialist AI, assessment, speaking, games, simulations, practice or other learning technology into Wistudi experiences.

Integration partners

Connect Wistudi with LMS, identity, data, reporting and organisation infrastructure.

Content partners

Publish curricula, programmes, resources and structured learning products through Wistudi.

Distribution partners

Bring Wistudi and partner capabilities into existing institutions, sectors, communities and markets.
