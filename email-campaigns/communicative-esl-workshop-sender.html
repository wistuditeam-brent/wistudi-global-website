The teaching layer

Build the learning journey, not just the activity.

Teachers have access to countless activities and tools, but choosing something simply because it is fun or engaging does not always mean it will support learning.

We’ll look at what learners need at each stage, how much support to provide, and how activities can move learners from becoming familiar with language to using it more independently.

The lesson gives us a concrete example, but the goal is transferable: clearer activity choices, better lesson flow, and more confident communication.
