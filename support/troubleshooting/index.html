Troubleshooting

Resolve loading, playback and access problems, protect unsaved work, and send a useful report to Wistudi Support.

Wistudi Support · Updated 9 November 2026
Quick answer

First record the error and protect unsaved work. Then check the account, link, connection and browser. Contact support with the affected page and the steps that reproduce the problem.

Before refreshing or closing the page

If you are writing a response or editing content, keep a copy of text you have not successfully saved or submitted. Check the save or submission message before leaving.

Record the exact error, the content title and what you were doing when the problem occurred. A screenshot of the error is helpful, but crop or hide unrelated personal or learner information.

Do not repeatedly confirm publishing, payment or submission actions while the result is unclear. Check whether the action completed first. If a credit balance or payment looks wrong, contact support with the transaction context rather than trying the same purchase again.

A page or video will not load

  1. Check whether other pages load on the same connection.
  2. After protecting unsaved work, reload the affected page.
  3. Try the page in an updated browser. If needed, compare with another browser or a private window to check whether an extension or browser setting is involved.
  4. If only one video fails, note whether it is an external video and copy the exact player message. A source video can become unavailable independently of Wistudi.
  5. If the platform displays a desktop-only notice, use a desktop or laptop browser and reopen the original link.

A private window may require you to sign in again. Do not clear browser storage or cookies as your first step: it can remove local session information and sign you out.

A lesson is locked or progress looks wrong

For an access message, check the original invitation and the account you are signed into. An expired link, access limit or permission restriction needs the sender or administrator to review it; changing browsers will not grant permission.

If progress or a result appears missing, check that you are using the same account and the same lesson. Note whether you completed the work as a guest, in a preview or while signed in. Record the activity name, approximate completion time and any confirmation you saw.

Do not restart an assessed activity just to test whether the record changes. Ask your teacher or support to review it if another attempt could affect your result.

Saving or publishing did not finish

Keep the page open while you check its status. Look for validation messages, required settings or a clear save confirmation. If the connection dropped, preserve unsaved text before retrying.

If a publishing action appears to have used credits but did not complete, send the content title, approximate time and error to support. Do not delete the content to try to reverse the action; deletion is not a credit-restoration request. The Credit Usage Policy explains how verified technical errors are reviewed.

Send a useful support report

Email support@wistudi.com or use the support enquiry form. Include:

  • Your account email and whether you were signed in or using guest access.
  • The page link, content title and affected activity.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
  • The approximate date, time and timezone.
  • Your device, browser and the exact error message.
  • A screenshot if it helps explain the problem.

Never send passwords, reset links, verification codes or full payment-card details. Share private content links only with the authorised sender or support.
