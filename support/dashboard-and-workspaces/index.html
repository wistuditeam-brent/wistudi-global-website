Getting started & your account

Dashboard and workspaces

Find Discover, My Learning Library, My Progress and your publishing workspaces, and understand locked navigation areas.

Wistudi Support · Updated 9 November 2026
Quick answer

Use Discover to find learning, My Learning Library to return to lessons and Publishing Workspace to create or manage your own content.

Choose the area for your task

Wistudi separates exploring, learning and publishing in the navigation. These are different views of your work, so a lesson you are taking and a lesson you are editing may appear in different places.

  • Discover: explore available XP Videos and Flows.
  • My Learning Library: return to video and Flow lessons associated with your learning.
  • My Progress: view the learning progress available for your account.
  • Publishing Workspace: find the XP Videos and Flows areas for creating, editing and managing your own content.
  • My Profile and Account Settings: use the account menu for your profile and account preferences.

Some navigation labels and options depend on the account and workspace you are using. A creator or administrator may see controls that a learner does not.

Return to a lesson

  1. Sign in with the account you used when you started the lesson or received the invitation.
  2. Open My Learning Library and look for the lesson title.
  3. If you cannot find it, open the original invitation or assignment link.
  4. Follow the access message or the lesson's start or continue action.

Do not use a creator's editing workspace as a shortcut to a learner assignment. The original invitation is the best starting point when you are unsure which content was shared with you.

Find your publishing work

For content you own or help manage, open the appropriate XP Videos or Flows area in Publishing Workspace. Check the title and content type before editing.

If your account offers a workspace selector, check that the intended personal or organisation workspace is selected. A change of workspace can change the content and permissions you see. Do not create a duplicate lesson until you have checked the account and workspace.

Understand locked navigation

Some sections unlock through onboarding. The navigation can show requirements such as completing a profile, choosing learning preferences, or creating content.

  1. Expand the locked section and read its requirements.
  2. Use the action shown for the requirement you want to complete.
  3. Finish and save the requested setup, then return to the navigation.

Completing onboarding does not grant organisation permissions or access to someone else's private content. If an access restriction remains, contact the person who invited you or support with the message you see.
