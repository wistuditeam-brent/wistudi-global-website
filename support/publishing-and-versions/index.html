Creating & publishing

Publish, update and manage versions

Check publishing requirements, distinguish workspace publication from Discover review, and prepare updates without confusing existing learners.

Wistudi Support · Updated 18 November 2026
Quick answer

Save and preview your content, complete the publishing requirements and review the confirmation. For Flows, workspace publication, Discover listing and template review are separate states.

Check the content before publishing

Save your draft, preview the learner experience and resolve the requirements shown in the builder. Confirm the title, instructions, answer configuration and resources before release.

  • XP Videos: review Publishing Requirements. The activity requirement varies with video length, and checkpoints and questions must be configured.
  • Flows: open Configure Flow and finish the required General Info, Content & Identity and Visuals & Branding fields. Use Save & Exit and resolve any remaining content warnings.

Saving a draft and publishing are different actions. A successful save does not mean a learner can already open the content.

Publish a Flow to your workspace

  1. Choose Publish when the builder requirements are complete.
  2. Review the Publish Flow confirmation and its available publishing options.
  3. Check the intended visibility and whether you are also requesting a Discover listing or submitting a template.
  4. Review the credit summary, included output and any acknowledgements.
  5. Confirm publication only when you are ready, then wait for the success message.

Workspace publication makes a published version available in your own workspace. It does not by itself mean the Flow is publicly listed in Discover.

Template submission has separate reuse permissions and acknowledgements. Do not select it simply to obtain a learner link. Read the Template Publishing & Remix Terms before granting reuse rights.

Understand Discover review

A Flow can be published in your workspace while a Discover submission is still under review. Use the available Discover publishing option or Available in Discover in configuration when you want to request a listing.

Check Review Status and the feedback shown. In Review is not approval. If changes are requested, address the reasons and required actions before resubmitting.

Where offered, Recall Submission cancels the pending review so you can edit again. A live, approved listing can have different controls: if the interface requires an administrator to remove it, contact an administrator rather than treating it as a normal draft.

XP Video publishing uses its own status and confirmation. Read the video interface directly instead of assuming the Flow review process applies unchanged.

Edit with existing learners in mind

Before editing published content, check its current version, learners and review status. The available action can be Edit, Update, Edit Draft or Create New Version.

In Manage Flow Versions, a new editable version is a draft copy. Creating that draft does not itself replace the current learner experience. Learners stay on their existing version while you prepare the update.

If there are no enrolled learners, the interface may offer direct editing of the published version. An existing draft, a pending review or a live Discover listing can restrict the options. Follow the explanation shown for that Flow.

Before publishing updates, review how they affect the current release and any review requirements. Do not assume that changing answers, points or assessed tasks has the same effect as correcting explanatory text. Check the confirmation and ask support before a change that could affect an active assessment.

Review credit confirmations

Publishing pages, eligible videos, new versions or selected advanced options can use credits. Review the total and any breakdown displayed before confirming. Use the amount shown in the confirmation for your action, as costs depend on what you publish.

If publication appears to fail after credits were deducted, keep the content and note the time and error. Do not repeatedly confirm the action or delete the content to try to restore credits. Contact support and refer to the Credit Usage Policy.

Check access after publication

Open the published item from the workspace, check its status and obtain a learner link through Share. Test the intended access route before distributing it widely.

Archiving, moving to trash, deleting, removing a Discover listing and deleting a draft version are different actions. Read the confirmation for the action you choose; do not use deletion as a shortcut to hide or update content used by learners.
