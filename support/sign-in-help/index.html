Sign-in and account recovery

Sign in, reset a forgotten password, find verification emails and get help with restricted or inaccessible accounts.

Wistudi Support · Updated 9 November 2026
Quick answer

Use the email address and sign-in method connected to your account. For a forgotten password, choose Forgot your password? and request a reset link.

Sign in to the correct account

  1. Open Sign In.
  2. For email sign-in, enter your account email and password, then choose Sign In.
  3. If you registered using Google, use the Google sign-in option with the same Google account.
  4. Complete any verification or account prompts shown.
  5. If you are trying to open shared learning, return to the original invitation link after signing in.

Check for typing mistakes and unwanted spaces in your email address. If you use more than one email or Google account, make sure you are using the identity that received the invitation or owns the work you need.

Reset a forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in page, choose Forgot your password?, or open password recovery.
  2. Enter your account email address and choose Send Reset Link.
  3. Open the reset email and follow the complete link.
  4. Enter your new password in New Password and repeat it in Confirm New Password.
  5. Choose Reset Password. Once the success message appears, return to sign-in and use your new password.

Follow the password requirements shown by the form. If the link is invalid, expired or missing its reset token, choose Request a new link and use the new email. Do not forward a reset link to anyone else.

If an account email has not arrived

  • Check that the email address you entered is correct and that you can receive mail in that inbox.
  • Look in spam, junk and any filtered mail folders.
  • Use Resend reset link or Resend verification email on the relevant screen.
  • Open the message for the action you just requested. An email-verification link and a password-reset link have different purposes.

If the message still does not arrive, contact support with your account email, the action you requested and the approximate time. Do not include your password or a full reset or verification link.

If your account is restricted or suspended

A password reset does not remove an account restriction. If Wistudi displays a suspension or access message, read the explanation and contact your organisation's administrator or Wistudi Support as appropriate.

Include the exact message and the email address on your account. Avoid creating another account to bypass a restriction; it can separate your learning and content from the original account.

If you cannot access the email inbox linked to your account, ask support for help. Do not assume a new account can inherit your previous content or records.
