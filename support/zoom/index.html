Zoom & Training Rooms

Using Zoom with Wistudi

Connect Zoom, host or join a Training Room, fix common problems and manage your connection and data.

Wistudi Support · Updated 18 November 2026
Quick answer

Connect your Zoom account in My Space → Training Rooms, create a room with a Flow, and share the room link with your learners. Need help? Go to Zoom troubleshooting or contact Wistudi Support.

Requirements

  • Hosts: a Wistudi account and a Zoom account that you are authorised to connect. A managed Zoom account may require administrator approval.
  • Learners: a Wistudi account and the Training Room link supplied by the host. If the room asks for Zoom authentication, follow the sign-in instructions or ask the host about the account required.
  • A current desktop version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, a stable internet connection, and permission to use your microphone and camera when needed. Features can vary by browser. See Zoom’s browser support.
  • A free Zoom account normally limits meetings to 40 minutes; a paid host licence is recommended for longer classes. See Zoom’s meeting time limits.

The meeting opens inside the Wistudi browser page. Use a desktop or laptop for this Training Room guide. Read the requirements for younger learners before inviting children.

Add the app

  1. Sign in to Wistudi and open My Space → Training Rooms.
  2. Click Connect Zoom.
  3. Sign in to the Zoom account you want to use for hosting. Review the permissions and click Allow. If your organisation requires app approval, ask its Zoom administrator.
  4. Return to Training Rooms and check that your Zoom account is shown as connected.

If authorisation fails, follow connection troubleshooting. Connecting lets Wistudi manage the Zoom meetings used by your Training Rooms. Read the Zoom privacy disclosure and Zoom integration terms before connecting.

Use the app

Host a class

  1. In My Space → Training Rooms, click Create Training Room.
  2. Choose a Flow, enter a name and choose when the room is open. Check the opening time before sending invitations.
  3. Copy the room link and send it to your learners.
  4. Click Enter. Zoom opens inside Wistudi and the Flow is shown alongside the meeting. Allow microphone and camera access if your browser asks.
  5. Keep the host in the meeting while learners join.
  6. When the class is over, click End room. The Zoom meeting ends for everyone.

Join as a learner

  1. Open the host’s Training Room link and sign in to Wistudi.
  2. Join while the room is open and the host is in the meeting. Follow any Zoom authentication or admission instructions shown.
  3. Check your microphone, camera and audio controls. Use the Flow alongside the video session.

If a learner cannot enter, see room access troubleshooting.

Remove the app

You can remove the connection from either Wistudi or Zoom:

  1. In Wistudi: open My Space → Training Rooms → Disconnect Zoom.
  2. In Zoom: sign in at marketplace.zoom.us, open Manage → Added Apps, select Wistudi, then click Remove and confirm. If your Marketplace shows My Library, find Wistudi there and choose Remove from its actions menu. See Zoom’s app removal instructions.

When the app is removed, Wistudi deletes your stored Zoom tokens. Existing Training Rooms can no longer start until you connect Zoom again. End an active class before disconnecting; disconnection should not be used in place of End room.

Removing the connection is separate from deleting your Wistudi account or requesting deletion of other records. It does not delete your Zoom account or independently erase data held by Zoom. See Zoom data retention and deletion, or email support@wistudi.com to request access or deletion. Reconnect through Connect Zoom when you want to host again.

Zoom troubleshooting

I cannot connect Zoom or authorisation was denied

Check that you are signed in to the intended Wistudi and Zoom accounts. Retry Connect Zoom, review the permissions, and complete the Zoom authorisation. If Zoom says administrator approval is needed, contact your organisation’s Zoom administrator. If a browser blocks a required sign-in window or redirect, allow it for Wistudi and Zoom and retry. Do not disable your browser’s security protections globally.

My microphone, camera or audio does not work

Check the meeting’s mute, camera and audio controls and select the intended devices. Allow microphone and camera access for Wistudi in your browser’s site settings and in your operating system. Close other applications using the same device, check your speaker volume and try headphones. If the problem continues, note the error and try an updated supported desktop browser.

A learner cannot enter the Training Room

Check the original room link, the room’s opening time, the learner’s Wistudi sign-in, and that the host is currently in the meeting. The host should check that Zoom is still connected. If Zoom requires authentication or admission, follow the displayed instructions; signing in to Wistudi does not override Zoom account or meeting restrictions.

The meeting ends or the connection stops working

Check whether the host ended the room, the host’s Zoom plan reached its meeting limit, or the internet connection dropped. Reopen the original room link if the room remains open. If Wistudi reports an expired or disconnected Zoom connection, the host should end any active class and reconnect Zoom from Training Rooms. Reconnection does not restore a meeting that has already ended.

Zoom stays on a loading or error screen

Record the exact message, then reload the room and check your internet connection. Try a current supported desktop browser. If it works on a different network or device, ask your organisation’s IT team to check its Zoom access rules. Send persistent problems to Wistudi Support with the steps that reproduce them.

Common questions

Does each learner need to connect a Zoom account?

The connection steps above are for the host. Learners use the Training Room link and sign in to Wistudi. Additional Zoom sign-in requirements depend on the meeting and account settings; if prompted, follow those instructions or ask the host.

Do I need the Zoom desktop application?

This integration opens the meeting in Wistudi’s desktop browser page. Follow the browser joining instructions rather than assuming that installing the Zoom desktop application will fix a room access problem.

Where are recordings?

Wistudi does not store Zoom meeting recordings. If recording is available and enabled under the host’s Zoom plan and settings, ask the host about its location, permissions and retention in Zoom. A host who records or enables transcription must provide the required notices and obtain any necessary consent. Do not assume a Training Room is recorded.

Privacy and data

The Zoom section of Wistudi’s Privacy Policy explains connection information, meeting metadata, participant display names, retention and data requests. Live meeting media is processed through Zoom; see the Zoom Privacy Statement. Wistudi account, Flow and other platform records remain covered by the wider Wistudi Privacy Policy.

Read the Zoom integration terms for host responsibilities. You can disconnect the app without closing your Wistudi account.

Younger learners

Zoom’s Terms of Service restrict use by children under 16 to use through a School Subscriber using Zoom for Education (K-12). Before inviting under-16 learners, the school or organisation must confirm that its Zoom arrangement meets those requirements and that the necessary notices and permissions are in place. Wistudi’s supervised-access rules or parental permission alone do not establish Zoom eligibility.

Contact support

Wistudi Zoom support

For help with Zoom inside Wistudi, email support@wistudi.com or send a support enquiry.

Support hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Vietnam time (Indochina Time, UTC+7; Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh). Vietnam does not observe daylight saving time.

First response: within one business day. Messages received outside support hours are handled from the next business day. This is the time for an initial reply; resolving an issue may take longer.

Include your Wistudi account email, the affected Training Room link, the date, time and timezone, your device and browser, the steps you took and the exact error. A screenshot can help; hide unrelated personal information. Never send passwords, meeting passcodes or Zoom tokens.

For Zoom account billing or a problem outside Wistudi, visit Zoom Support.
