Zoom & Training Rooms

Using Zoom with Wistudi

Connect Zoom, run live classes with a Flow in Training Rooms, and disconnect the app when you no longer need it.

Wistudi Support · Updated 18 November 2026
Quick answer

Connect your Zoom account in My Space → Training Rooms, create a room with a Flow, and share the room link with your learners.

Requirements

  • A Wistudi account.
  • A Zoom account. A free Zoom account normally ends meetings after 40 minutes; a paid Zoom licence is recommended for classes. See Zoom’s meeting time limits.
  • A desktop browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Add the app

  1. Sign in to Wistudi and open My Space → Training Rooms.
  2. Click Connect Zoom.
  3. Sign in to Zoom and click Allow.
  4. The Training Rooms page now shows your Zoom account as connected.

Use the app

  1. In My Space → Training Rooms, click Create Training Room.
  2. Choose a Flow, enter a name and choose when the room is open.
  3. Copy the room link and send it to your learners.
  4. Click Enter. Zoom starts inside Wistudi and the Flow is shown next to the video.
  5. Learners open the link, sign in to Wistudi and join the meeting. They can join only while the host is in the meeting.
  6. When the class is over, click End room. The Zoom meeting ends for everyone.

Remove the app

You can remove the connection from either Wistudi or Zoom:

  • In Wistudi: open My Space → Training Rooms → Disconnect Zoom.
  • In Zoom: sign in at marketplace.zoom.us, then open Manage → Added Apps → Wistudi → Remove.

If your Zoom Marketplace shows My Library instead, find Wistudi there and choose Remove from its actions menu. See Zoom’s app removal instructions.

When the app is removed, Wistudi deletes your stored Zoom tokens. Existing Training Rooms can no longer start until you connect Zoom again.
