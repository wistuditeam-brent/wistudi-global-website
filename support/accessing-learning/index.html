Learning on Wistudi

Find and access learning

Open an invitation or shared lesson, understand guest and signed-in access, and resolve unavailable or expired links.

Wistudi Support · Updated 9 November 2026
Quick answer

Open the original lesson link and follow its access instructions. A private link can have restrictions, and signing in does not automatically grant access to every lesson.

Open a shared lesson or invitation

  1. Use the complete link supplied by your teacher, trainer or publisher.
  2. Check the title and creator on the invitation or content page.
  3. If asked, choose Sign In or create an account. Use the account intended for the invitation.
  4. After signing in or finishing account setup, return to the original link if the lesson has not reopened.
  5. Choose the start or continue action displayed and read the lesson instructions before beginning.

If your teacher sent a replacement link, use that link rather than an older bookmark. Do not publish a private invitation link in a public group or on social media.

Find learning without an invitation

Use Discover to explore content available there. If you have already started a lesson, look in My Learning Library or return to its original link.

Private or assigned content does not have to appear in Discover. If you cannot find a lesson in search, ask the person who shared it for the correct access link instead of looking for a similar title.

Understand guest access

Some shared experiences offer Continue as Guest; others require sign-in. The choices shown depend on the content and its sharing settings.

Guest progress can be limited to the browser session, and features such as saved account progress or reports may be unavailable. If the learning is an assignment or your teacher needs a recorded result, sign in with the intended account before starting. Do not assume work completed as a guest will transfer to your account later.

A preview or guest view is not the same as being enrolled or having full learner access. Follow the invitation's instructions if it asks you to sign in.

If the lesson will not open

  • Sign-in required: sign in, then reopen the original invitation.
  • Wrong account or workspace: check the signed-in email and the intended workspace where available.
  • Invalid, expired or access-limit message: ask the sender to check the link and provide new access if appropriate. Repeated refreshing does not renew a link.
  • Content unavailable: the publisher may need to check its publication or sharing settings. Ask them before assuming it is a device problem.
  • Locked pages or activities: read the requirements shown in the lesson. Access to one page does not necessarily mean every page is open.

If the issue continues, send support the content title, page link and exact message. Send private links only to the sender or support, and do not include passwords or reset links.
