Getting started & your account

Your account and profile

Create and verify your account, find your profile and account settings, and understand which details can be visible to others.

Wistudi Support · Updated 9 November 2026
Quick answer

Use your own account and an email address you can access. My Profile is for your profile; Account Settings is for account preferences. Review visibility before sharing a profile.

Register with an email you control

  1. Open the registration page.
  2. Enter your first name, last name, email address and password in the fields shown.
  3. Review the information and choose Create Account.
  4. Open the verification email and follow the link to confirm your email address.
  5. Continue to sign in and complete the profile or onboarding steps shown.

If you already have an account, use Sign In. Creating another account can leave invitations, learning and published work associated with different identities.

Independent registration is for users aged 16 or over. Younger learners need an approved supervised arrangement. A parent, guardian or authorised organisation should contact Wistudi if they need help arranging access.

Verify your email address

Verification confirms that you can access the email address on your account. Check that the address displayed on the verification screen is correct, then look in your inbox and spam or junk folder.

Use Resend verification email if needed. If a verification link is invalid or expired, choose Request a new link and use the newly issued email. Copying only part of the URL can prevent it from working.

Never send your verification link or password to another person. Support may ask for your account email and the error message, but does not need your password.

Find your profile and settings

Open your account menu and look for My Profile or Account Settings.

  • My Profile: view your profile and the editing options available to you. Review your display information before sharing your profile.
  • Account Settings: manage the account preferences and settings available for your account.
  • Profile setup: if onboarding asks you to complete your profile, follow that prompt. You can also open profile settings while signed in.

After editing, use the save or confirmation control shown and check that the update succeeds before leaving. A setting labelled Coming soon is not a promise that the associated service is active.

Understand visibility and protect your account

Your public profile and your private account records are different. Profile information may be displayed according to your visibility and publishing settings; that does not make private learner progress, assessments or transactions public.

Only include contact details or personal information you intend to share in a public profile. Review the Privacy Policy for how Wistudi handles personal information.

Keep your password private, use a password you do not reuse elsewhere, and sign out on a shared device. If you no longer control the account email or suspect someone else has accessed your account, contact support@wistudi.com before creating a replacement account.
