Getting started & your account

Getting started with Wistudi

Choose where to begin: open shared learning, explore Discover or start publishing with XP Videos and Flows.

Wistudi Support · Updated 9 November 2026
Quick answer

If someone has invited you to a lesson, start with their link. If you want to explore or publish, sign in and use Discover or your publishing workspace.

Choose your starting point

You do not need to build anything to take part in learning. Start with the task you came to do:

  • Complete a lesson: open the link from your teacher, trainer or publisher. Follow the invitation and sign-in instructions on that page.
  • Explore learning: open Discover to browse available XP Videos and Flows.
  • Create learning: use the XP Videos or Flows area of your publishing workspace. Begin with one short lesson rather than a complete course.
  • Attend an event: use the event's registration or joining link. Event registration and access to a separate lesson are different actions; follow the instructions supplied by your host.

Create or access your account

  1. Open Create an account. If you already have an account, choose Sign In instead.
  2. For email registration, enter your first name, last name, email address and password, then choose Create Account. Use an email inbox you can access.
  3. Look for the verification email and follow its link. If it is missing, check spam or junk and use Resend verification email on the verification screen.
  4. Complete any profile or onboarding requirements shown in your account.
  5. If you arrived through an invitation, return to the original invitation link after signing in.

Account creation does not give access to every private lesson, organisation or event. Access still depends on the invitation, account permissions and the content's availability.

Understand XP Videos and Flows

An XP Video adds activities and checkpoints to a video. A checkpoint can ask you to answer, reflect or complete a task while watching.

A Flow brings pages, content and activities into a learning sequence. It can combine explanations, videos, questions and resources in one experience.

If you are learning, follow the instructions and requirements displayed in the experience. If you are publishing, choose the format that fits the learning: video-led learning suits an XP Video; a sequence of mixed content suits a Flow.

If part of the platform is locked

Some navigation areas have onboarding requirements. Read the requirement shown beside the locked section and use its action to continue. Do not create another account to try to unlock it.

A message that your account or content has restricted access needs a different response: return to your invitation, contact the person who shared the content, or ask Wistudi Support. See Dashboard and workspaces for help choosing the right area.
