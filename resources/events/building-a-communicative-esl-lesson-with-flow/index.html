About the workshop

Build the learning journey, not just the activity.

Teachers have access to countless tools and activities, but choosing something simply because it is fun or engaging does not always mean it will support learning.

In this practical workshop, we will look at how to build a communicative ESL lesson that helps students feel more confident using and speaking English. We will explore how different activities can support learners as they move from becoming familiar with language to using it more independently.

You will also see a complete communicative lesson built in Wistudi, consider alternative ways to achieve the same learning purpose, and explore how the same lesson can be adapted for offline teaching and a printable worksheet.

What you will explore

Purpose first. Activity second.

The session is designed around the decisions teachers make as learners move towards more independent communication.

Choose activities with intention

Look beyond whether an activity is engaging and ask what learning purpose it serves at that point in the lesson.

Adjust learner support

Consider how much guidance learners need as they move from recognition and practice towards more independent use.

Explore different routes

Brainstorm alternative activities that can achieve the same learning objective rather than relying on one fixed format.

Connect online and offline teaching

See how the same learning journey can support interactive delivery and be adapted into printable classroom materials.
