Zoom integration

Using Zoom with Wistudi

Connect your Zoom account, choose a Flow and bring your learners together in a Training Room.

Requirements

  • A Wistudi account. Hosts also need a Zoom account they are authorised to connect; a managed account may require administrator approval.
  • A free Zoom account normally limits meetings to 40 minutes; a paid host licence is recommended for longer classes.
  • A current desktop version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, an internet connection, and microphone and camera permissions when needed.

Add the app

  1. Sign in to Wistudi and open My Space → Training Rooms.
  2. Click Connect Zoom.
  3. Sign in to the intended Zoom account, review the permissions and click Allow. If approval is required, ask your organisation’s Zoom administrator.
  4. The page now shows your Zoom account as connected.

Having trouble connecting? See Zoom connection troubleshooting.

Use the app

  1. In My Space → Training Rooms, click Create Training Room.
  2. Choose a Flow, enter a name and choose when the room is open.
  3. Copy the room link and send it to your learners.
  4. Click Enter. Zoom starts inside Wistudi and the Flow is shown next to the video.
  5. Learners open the link, sign in to Wistudi and join while the room is open and the host is in the meeting. Follow any additional Zoom authentication or admission instructions shown.
  6. When the class is over, click End room. The Zoom meeting ends for everyone.

Remove the app

  • In Wistudi: My Space → Training Rooms → Disconnect Zoom.
  • In Zoom: sign in at marketplace.zoom.us → Manage → Added Apps → Wistudi → Remove.

When the app is removed, Wistudi deletes your stored Zoom tokens. Existing Training Rooms can no longer start until you connect Zoom again. End an active class before disconnecting.

For the current Zoom Marketplace menu, reconnection and what happens to other records, read the full removal instructions and data retention and deletion guidance.

Troubleshooting

The Zoom support guide covers connection and authorisation, microphone, camera and audio, joining a room, and meeting limits and disconnections.

Privacy and terms

Review how Wistudi handles Zoom data, the Zoom integration terms, and the Zoom Privacy Statement. Live meeting media is processed through Zoom; Wistudi does not store Zoom meeting recordings.

Support

Contact us through the Wistudi Support Centre or email support@wistudi.com.

Support hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Vietnam time (Indochina Time, UTC+7; Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh). Vietnam does not observe daylight saving time.

First response: within one business day. Messages received outside support hours are handled from the next business day. This is the time for an initial reply; resolving an issue may take longer.

For setup and troubleshooting, visit the Zoom support guide or send a support enquiry. Include your room link and error message; never send passwords, meeting passcodes or Zoom tokens.
