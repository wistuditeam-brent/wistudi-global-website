Why Wistudi for organisations

One connected environment instead of a stitched-together workflow.

The point is not that every organisation should replace every system. It is that learning, publishing and management should not become fragmented just because several tools are involved.

Common multi-tool setup
–Separate authoring and publishing tools
–Learning management handled elsewhere
–Printable workflows rebuilt separately
–Video learning sits in another workflow
–Community and discovery are disconnected
–Reporting is spread across systems
Wistudi connected approach
Publishing + learning + management around one core
✓Create and publish in one environment
✓Learning management built around the content
✓Interactive + printable remain connected
✓XP Video and Flows belong to the same ecosystem
✓Social learning and discovery can live alongside formal learning
✓Use independently or integrate when needed
