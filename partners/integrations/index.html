Partner integration guide

Partner integrations in Wistudi

How external learning tools, content platforms and LMS products can connect with Wistudi, and how we are designing those integrations to remain part of the learning experience.

For: Product, partnership and engineering teams Updated September 2026 Technical overview, not an implementation specification
Early partner applications are open for the 15 October 2026 integration launch. We are currently discussing first integrations and pilots with selected EdTech, content and learning-platform teams.
Apply as an early partner

Wistudi is built around complete learning experiences rather than isolated activities. That shapes how we approach integrations. The aim is not simply to add another external link. Where the partner product allows it, we want the experience to become part of the lesson itself.

That can mean a partner activity appearing directly inside a Wistudi Flow, sitting alongside Wistudi content and activities, and returning completion or score information without forcing the learner through unnecessary login and navigation steps.

Our preferred integration keeps the learner in context. A partner product should feel like part of the Flow, not a separate destination the learner repeatedly leaves Wistudi to visit.

Starting context: what a Flow and Block are

A Flow is the complete learning experience in Wistudi. It can be a focused lesson or a larger sequence containing explanation, media, interaction, assessment and external content.

Flows are built from Blocks. A Block is a focused unit within that experience. It may contain text, video, an interactive activity, an assessment, a resource or a partner application.

For integrations, the Block matters because it is where another product can become part of the wider lesson rather than sit beside it as an unrelated link.

FlowThe complete learning experience that brings different content, activities and tools together in sequence.
BlockA focused unit inside a Flow. Partner experiences are intended to appear as Blocks where the technical model supports it.
Interactive + printableWistudi connects digital learning with printable output. The partner activity may stay digital while the surrounding Flow can also support worksheets and other printable resources.

Why the inline experience matters

A learner should not feel that the lesson stopped because another application opened. When technically possible, the partner experience should remain inside the Flow and preserve the surrounding learning context.

Some products will need a new-tab launch because of authentication, browser security or their own architecture. That can still be supported. It is simply not the preferred experience when a strong inline option is available.

The same principle applies to authentication and results. If the learner is already in Wistudi, we prefer not to ask for another login unless it is necessary. If an activity produces a meaningful completion state or score, we prefer that result to return to Wistudi where the partner supports it.

Who we are interested in partnering with

We are especially interested in products that become more useful when placed inside a wider lesson, curriculum or guided learning sequence.

  • Interactive learning tools: simulations, manipulatives, creative tools, practice environments and specialist applications.
  • Assessment and practice: quizzes, diagnostics, adaptive practice and specialist assessment products.
  • Content and publishers: reading, curriculum, media and educational content providers.
  • Subject-specific EdTech: products for maths, STEM, literacy, languages, coding and other specialist areas.
  • AI learning applications: AI products with a clear learning purpose that can be placed intentionally inside a lesson.
  • LMS and learning platforms: platforms that want to launch Wistudi content, exchange results or explore a two-way workflow.

Integration options

Partners do not need to implement every level. In many cases, the right approach is to begin with the smallest integration that creates useful value and extend it only when there is a clear reason.

Level 1: Simple Embed

This is the fastest route when a partner already has a page or activity that can be shown securely in an embedded view. Wistudi loads that experience inside the lesson. There is no security handshake, learner identity transfer or result capture at this level.

Level 2: Connected Access and Results

This level adds trusted access and basic result handling. Wistudi generates a signed, short-lived token containing a pseudonymous learner identifier and the relevant Flow and step references. The partner verifies the token before granting access.

When the activity is finished, the partner can return completion or score information. The intention is to reduce friction without sending names, email addresses or other personal information by default.

Level 3: Content Workflow Integration

This is intended for partners where creators need to select or create content rather than paste a link. The partner hosts the selection or editing screen, Wistudi launches it, and the chosen activity reference is returned to the relevant Block.

Level 4: Extended Data Sharing

Some partner products genuinely need more context, such as role, class or group information. These fields can be considered case by case and should be limited to what the partner experience actually needs.

Level 5: LTI 1.3 and custom connections

This level covers more advanced cases, including the reverse direction where another LMS or learning platform wants to launch Wistudi content.

LTI 1.3 direction
  • Launch: standard LTI 1.3 with OIDC and JWKS-based signing.
  • Content selection: Deep Linking for selecting specific Wistudi content.
  • Grade return: Assignment and Grade Services where applicable.
  • Custom cases: dedicated APIs or webhooks can be scoped if the normal launch model does not fit.

What we look at before scoping an integration

A more complicated integration is not automatically a better one. We start with the learner experience and the practical value created for both products.

01
Can the experience work inside a Flow?We first look at whether it can be embedded or launched while preserving lesson context.
02
Does the learner need another login?If so, we assess whether a trusted launch can remove that friction.
03
What should return to Wistudi?For some products, completion is enough. Others may need a score or another agreed result.
04

05

Early partner process

The early partner programme is intended to identify the right integration level before either team commits to unnecessary build work.

  1. Fit conversation. We review the partner product, audience, use case and intended learner experience.
  2. Experience mapping. We establish where the product should sit inside a Flow and how a creator would add it.
  3. Technical scope. Engineering teams confirm launch method, security, result handling and any required data.
  4. Pilot and review. Selected partners can move into a limited test before a wider rollout.

Common questions

Do we need to support LTI 1.3 to become a Wistudi partner?

No. LTI 1.3 is one advanced pathway. A partner experience inside Wistudi may begin with a simple embed or connected launch instead.

Does our product have to run inside an iframe?

No, but inline delivery is our preferred learner experience. If security or product architecture requires a separate launch, we can assess that during scoping.

Do we have to return grades?

No. Some partner experiences are useful without grading. An activity may return completion only, a score where one exists, or no result at the simplest integration level.

Can teachers select partner content directly from Wistudi?

That is the planned direction for Content Workflow Integration. The intended model is a partner-hosted selection or creation screen that returns the selected activity reference to Wistudi.

Can Wistudi content launch inside our LMS?

That is part of the advanced reverse-direction integration scope. The planned model uses standard LTI 1.3, including Deep Linking and grade return where required.
