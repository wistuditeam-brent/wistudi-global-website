Starting context

What Wistudi is integrating into

Wistudi is a learning publishing and delivery platform built around Flows. A Flow is a structured learning experience that can combine explanation, media, interaction, assessment and partner content in one sequence.

Flows are made from Blocks. A Block is a focused piece of the experience, such as an activity, video, text resource, assessment, external tool or partner application. For integrations, the Block is the point where a partner experience can become part of the lesson itself.

Wistudi also connects interactive digital learning with printable resources. That matters for partners because a digital experience may sit inside the online Flow while the wider learning journey can also extend into worksheets and other printable material. Where it is useful, we can explore stable links or QR entry points back into partner experiences from print.

01Flow

The complete learning experience. It can be short or substantial and can contain Wistudi Blocks, partner content, assessment and supporting resources.

02Block

A focused activity or content unit within a Flow. Partner integrations are designed to appear as Blocks so they sit naturally within the surrounding lesson.

03Interactive + printable

Wistudi is designed for digital learning and printable output to coexist, so a partner integration can form one part of a wider online and offline publishing workflow.

A core integration principle

Keep the learning experience together.

A learner should not feel that the lesson stopped because another product opened. Our first question is therefore not only whether two systems can technically connect. It is whether the connection can become part of a coherent Flow.

Some partners will need a new-tab launch for security or product reasons, and we can support that where necessary. Our preference, however, is an embedded or otherwise closely connected experience that preserves context and reduces unnecessary login, navigation and result-handling steps.

Inline where possibleThe partner experience should run inside the Flow rather than sending the learner through a chain of separate pages.
Low-friction accessConnected integrations should avoid a second learner login when the partner's security model allows it.
Results in contextIf an activity produces completion or a score, we want that result available to the surrounding Wistudi learning experience where supported.
Creator-friendly setupOver time, creators should be able to select or create partner content without copying URLs between systems.
Minimum necessary dataPartner integrations should request only the learner, class or role information genuinely needed for the experience to work.

Integration options

Start with the level that solves the real problem

Partners do not need to implement every level. Most can start with a simple experience and add access, results or content workflows only when they create clear value.

Level 1

Simple embed

Your activity or content is displayed inside a Wistudi lesson as an embedded page. This is the fastest route when the experience already works securely in an iframe and does not require connected login or result return.

In developmentNo security handshake, learner identity transfer or result capture is required at this level.
Level 2

Connected access and results

Wistudi launches the partner experience with a signed, short-lived token so the learner can be recognised without a separate login. The partner can then return completion or score information to Wistudi.

In developmentDesigned for pseudonymous learner access, signed launch validation and completion or score return.
Level 3

Content workflow integration

A Wistudi creator can select existing partner content, or potentially create and edit it, through a launcher hosted by the partner. The chosen activity is then stored directly against the relevant Block.

PlannedThe intended direction is a creator workflow that avoids copying links between products. Final implementation is confirmed with each partner.
Level 4

Extended data sharing

When an activity genuinely needs more than a pseudonymous learner ID, specific class, learner, group or role information can be considered as an add-on to the connected launch.

Planned add-onAdditional information is considered case by case and should be limited to what the partner experience actually needs.
Level 5

LTI 1.3, reverse launch and custom connections

For platforms that want to launch Wistudi content from their own LMS or learning environment, the planned direction is standard LTI 1.3 with OIDC, JWKS, Deep Linking and Assignment and Grade Services where required. Non-standard API or webhook integrations can also be scoped for specific partner needs.

Scoped per partnerThis pathway is intended for advanced or reverse-direction integrations and is not required for a normal inline partner Block.
LTI and interoperability

Familiar standards, without making the page a technical manual

For executive and product teams, the important point is that Wistudi is being designed to work with established education interoperability patterns. Engineering details are confirmed during partner scoping.

Partner content inside Wistudi

The standard Wistudi partner path is built around an inline Block. Depending on the partner, that may begin as a simple embed or use a connected launch that handles learner access and returns completion or score data.

  • Typical objective: keep the learner in the Flow
  • Access: simple URL or signed connected launch
  • Results: completion or score where supported
  • Creator workflow: content picker or create/edit launcher as a later level

Wistudi inside another platform

Where an LMS or partner platform wants to launch Wistudi content, our advanced integration direction uses standard LTI 1.3. This is the model familiar to teams that already work with systems such as Canvas, Moodle or Brightspace.

  • Launch: LTI 1.3 with OIDC and JWKS-based signing
  • Content selection: Deep Linking for specific Wistudi content
  • Grade return: Assignment and Grade Services where applicable
  • Scope: planned and confirmed individually with partner engineering teams
Partner readiness

What we look at first

A good integration is not automatically the most complicated one. We start with the learner experience and the value created for both products.

01
Can the experience work inside a Flow?We assess whether it can be embedded or launched in a way that preserves lesson context.
02
Does the learner need another login?If so, we look at whether connected access, LTI or another trusted launch can reduce that friction.
03
What should come back to Wistudi?For some tools, completion is enough. Others may need a score or another agreed result.
04
How should a creator add the content?We establish whether a URL is enough or whether a content picker, Deep Linking or a creator workflow would materially improve the experience.
05
What data is genuinely required?We prefer pseudonymous access and add only the specific learner or class information needed for the partner experience.
06
Does the integration improve a wider learning journey?We prioritise partnerships where the combined experience is more useful than simply placing two products next to each other.
Early partner process

From first conversation to integration scope

The early partner programme is intended to identify the right integration level before either team commits to unnecessary build work.

STEP 01

STEP 02

Common questions

Before you contact us

Do we need to support LTI 1.3 to become a Wistudi partner?

No. LTI 1.3 is one advanced pathway, especially when another LMS or learning platform wants to launch Wistudi content. A partner experience inside a Wistudi Flow may begin with a simple embed or Wistudi's connected launch model instead.

Does our product have to run inside an iframe?

Not in every case, but inline delivery is our preferred learner experience. If security, browser policy or the product architecture requires a separate launch, we can assess that during scoping.

Do we have to return grades?

No. Some experiences are useful without grading. A partner activity may return completion only, a score where one exists, or no result at all at the simplest integration level.

Can teachers choose partner content directly inside Wistudi?

That is the planned Level 3 direction. The intended model is for a partner to host a selection or creation screen that Wistudi can launch and receive the selected activity reference from.

Can Wistudi content launch inside our LMS?

That is part of the advanced, reverse-direction integration scope. The planned model uses standard LTI 1.3, including Deep Linking and grade return where required.

What is currently live?

The partner programme is preparing for its 15 October 2026 launch. Simple Embed and Connected Access & Results are currently in development. Content Workflow Integration and extended data sharing are planned, while advanced custom and reverse-direction integrations are scoped per partner.
