# Support Centre content plan

## Scope

Build the Support Centre structure first. Do not publish full instructions until the current product workflow is verified. Credits may be explained without publishing specific prices, balances, package sizes, rates, seat quantities or credit amounts.

## Routes and navigation

- Hub: https://global.wistudi.com/support/
- Entry points: desktop navigation, mobile navigation, shared footer, homepage and Resources.
- Current topic links use /support/#guide-{slug}. These are working links to expandable outlines, not empty articles.
- The article URLs below are reserved for the content phase and must not be linked publicly until the matching pages exist.
- Contact: support@wistudi.com or /contact/?type=support.
- Existing legal URLs remain unchanged and authoritative for policy wording.

## Article template for the content phase

Reuse the Terms document shell: breadcrumb, category, title, short answer, reviewed date, prerequisites, verified steps, common problems, related guides and contact support. Use a desktop sticky contents list and a mobile contents disclosure above the body. Use real product screenshots only. Keep Resources editorial content separate from product support.

## Verification before filling guides

Check current names, routes and workflows; uploads and media limits; guest and learner access; publishing/version rules; active roles; reporting; events and room controls; parental controls; templates; presentation and printing availability. Never present an outlined or planned capability as available. Do not add billing timelines, refund promises or support response guarantees without confirmation.

## Recommended content order

1. Account access, navigation, accessing learning and troubleshooting.
2. XP Videos, Flows, blocks, publishing and sharing.
3. Credits and billing, aligned with the existing Credit Usage Policy and without numerical amounts.
4. Learner management, reports, events and permissions.
5. Templates, presentation/printing, community and remaining privacy guidance after feature verification.
