Wistudi combines a usage-based software model with a partner-led go-to-market strategy.
Educators can adopt Wistudi directly, but the primary route to scale is through EdTech
integrations, institutional partnerships and distribution channels.
Partners integrate their technology into Wistudi to extend how their products can be used
within broader learning experiences. In return, Wistudi gains new platform capabilities and
potential access to established educator, learner and institutional networks.
This means growth does not depend solely on acquiring individual users one at a time.
Each successful integration can strengthen the product while creating another route into
schools, universities and education organisations.
